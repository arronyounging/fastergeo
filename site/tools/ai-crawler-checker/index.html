FAQ

What happens if I block AI crawlers?

They can't read your content, so AI engines describe your category using other sources — usually competitors. If AI visibility matters, a blanket block is the most damaging robots.txt mistake.

Should I ever block them?

Some publishers block training crawlers (CCBot, Bytespider) while allowing citation crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot) — a legitimate trade-off between training-data control and answer visibility. Just make it a decision, not an accident.
