Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

The practice of making AI engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Doubao, DeepSeek — mention and cite your brand when users ask for recommendations. SEO optimizes for ranked links; GEO optimizes for being part of the answer itself.

Is FasterGEO free?

Yes. Apache-2.0, self-hosted. Bring your own engine API keys, or use manual sampling sheets with zero keys. Your data never leaves your machine.

Which engines are covered?

Eighteen across two markets: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity globally; GLM, Doubao, DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen, ERNIE and Spark in China via API — plus manual sheets for engines without public APIs, such as Baidu AI and Google AI Overviews.

Do I need API keys?

No. Site auditing needs none at all. Sampling works with any keys you have; engines without keys fall back to manual sheets that import into the same metrics pipeline.

Why should I trust these numbers?

Because you don't have to take them on faith. Every metric is publicly defined and traceable to the code that computes it — see the methodology. Unmeasured values render as unmeasured, never as zero. Verdicts ship with verbatim quotes. And every statistic on this page is footnoted to its source — the same fabrication gate the product enforces on your content applies to our marketing.

How is this different from AI visibility monitoring tools?

Three ways. It measures the head of the funnel — whether AI even knows your brand or confuses it with another company — with quoted evidence. It closes the loop: findings become tickets with machine-verifiable acceptance, re-checked automatically. And it's the only tool covering both China and global engines.
