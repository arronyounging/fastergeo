FAQ

Straight answers.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is a practice of making AI engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Doubao, DeepSeek — mention and cite your brand when users ask for recommendations. Unlike SEO, which optimizes for ranked links, GEO optimizes for being part of the answer itself.

Is it really free?

Yes. Apache-2.0, self-hosted. You bring your own engine API keys — or none, using manual sampling sheets. There is nothing to subscribe to and your data never leaves your machine.

Which engines are covered?

18 engines across two markets: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity globally; GLM, Doubao, DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen, ERNIE and Spark in China via API — plus manual sheets for Baidu AI, Nano AI, ChatGPT/Claude web and Google AI Overviews.

Do I need API keys?

No. Auditing needs zero keys. Sampling uses whatever keys you have; engines without keys fall back to manual sheets that import into the same pipeline.

How is this different from AI visibility monitors?

Three ways: it measures the head of the funnel (does AI even know you — or confuse you with someone else) with quoted evidence; it closes the loop with machine-verified tickets instead of suggestions; and it is the only tool covering both China and global engines.

Migrating from GeoLook?

fastergeo metrics --format geolook re-scores your existing GeoLook samples directly — zero migration needed.
